[ FIELD NOTE 001 ]

Yes, they're
really called boobies.

The name may come from the Spanish bobo—“silly”—because these curious seabirds were famously unafraid of sailors.

But there’s nothing silly about a bird that turns a 30-metre plunge into dinner.
